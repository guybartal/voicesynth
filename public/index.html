Connect your Azure Speech resource

Sign in with az login in your terminal. Set your Speech resource ID and region in .env, then restart the server. Your account needs the Cognitive Services Speech User role. Tokens stay on the server; no API key is used.

AZURE_SPEECH_RESOURCE_ID=/subscriptions/.../resourceGroups/.../providers/Microsoft.CognitiveServices/accounts/...
AZURE_SPEECH_REGION=your-resource-region
